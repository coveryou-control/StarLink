09 Rules used across the screens
Orange is a signal, not a surface Unread counts, mentions, the send button, the active tab. Never a message bubble background or a full panel.
Two bubble types only Yours: CY Grey 1E2028, white text, tail bottom-right. Theirs: white with a 1px hairline, tail bottom-left. Radius 16, tail corner 4.
Avatars carry status Initials on a tinted circle, 2px white ring on the presence dot. Green online, grey offline. 40px in lists, 32px in threads.
One elevation Panels are separated by 1px hairlines, not shadows. Only floating things (the mobile compose button, dialogs) get a shadow.
Desktop → mobile mapping Rail becomes bottom nav. List and thread become two pages. Right panel becomes a full-screen info page. Nothing is dropped.
Touch targets 44px minimum on mobile, 36px on desktop. Compose bar sits above the safe area with 22px bottom padding.

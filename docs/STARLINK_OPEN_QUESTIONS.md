## 0. Decision pass — 2026-08-31 (Project Head)

A single ruling set answering the register's outstanding business questions, plus the three
scope-boundary questions raised on 2026-08-30. **This section is the authority where it
disagrees with an older row below**; the affected rows are annotated in place.

### The four ownership rules, now frozen

> **StarLink** owns Conversation execution.
> **CCS PRO** owns Customer, Lead/Opportunity, Work, Policy, Claim, Booking and Consent truth.
> **HRMS / Central IAM** owns employee identity and access authority.
> **CY Brain** owns command, visibility, analytics and intelligence.
>
> *"Realtime is for experience. Durable state is for correctness."*
> *"Do not create a local permanent truth simply because the upstream system is not ready
> yet — use a replaceable adapter."*

These restate what the build already does. They are recorded because a future change that
would violate one now has to argue with a decision rather than with a preference.

### The pilot is now TWO phases, in sequence

| | Scope | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| **Phase 1** | Employee / internal communication only — identity, permissions, messaging, realtime, search, attachments, audit, multi-user behaviour | Deployment + an S3 bucket + a list of pilot employees |
| **Phase 2** | Controlled customer pilot on the CoverYou website, one or few teams first, then expand | Everything in Phase 1, **plus** approved business values, an SMS provider, and the customer attachment surface |

**The consequence worth stating plainly.** Internal conversations carry no case, no SLA, no
routing and no queue (BR-23, D-15). So Phase 1 depends on **none** of the business values
that gate Phase 2 — not categories, working hours, holidays, SLA targets, capacity, OTP or
customer contact ownership. That is why the split matters, and it is what puts Phase 1
within reach before any of those answers arrive.
